This Foreign Exchange Manual is compiled and issued by the Central Bank oF
_ Nigeria (CBN) pursuant to the powers conferred on if by the Foreign Exchange
(Monitoring & Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 17, of 1995 LFN Cap F34. The

General Public in processing foreign exchange transactions.

This edition has been revised in line with the provisions of the Act of 1995 and,
therefore, shall be used in conjunction with circulars issued by the CBN from time
to time.
