## More payment channels  means.. more business for you!

#### myfawry App

- Fawry provides a simple way to process payments anywhere and anytime to make things even more accessible.

- With just a click of a button, unit owners can settle their dues via the myfawry App.

- Business owners get up-to-date payment reports that outline payment methods and help keep track of unit owners’ dues.

- Never to miss a single payment. Set up your recurring bills and payment method, and just sit back and relax while we handle everything.
- Add up to 3 different cards: Debit, Credit and Prepaid


#### Fawry Plus Stores

- Settle your payments via any of our Fawry Plus branches.

- Find all available payment options at the tips of your fingers, easy and efficient, on the spot.

- Pay with debit, credit or prepaid cards.


#### Fawry Retail  Network

- Pay your bills at endless Fawry retail network, from kiosks to supermarkets, using Fawry’s POS.

- Fawry’s POS Systems have many features you and your clients will definitely enjoy.

- Our optimized point-of-sale solution will enhance every customer journey using the latest technology.

- Every terminal meets PCI compliance and is certified by Visa and Mastercard.

#### Banking  Channels

Fawry services are also available through:

- **ATMs**
In collaboration with certain banks, Fawry offers a cardless service, where you have access to various Fawry services through the banks’ ATMs.

- **Mobile Wallets**
In collaboration with certain banks, Fawry services are offered through the banks’ digital wallets.
