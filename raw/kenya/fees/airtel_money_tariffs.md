### Airtel Money Tariff

As Airtel, we take great pleasure in looking out for the well-being of our customers and in the wake of the COVID – 19 pandemic, we have made some changes to assist our customers access Airtel Money services conveniently and at reduced costs.

We have waived all person to person transaction fees for Airtel Money customers across all the bands. Further to this, in boosting and supporting the SME and micro business enterprise, together with the Central Bank of Kenya we have also revised the transaction limits for Airtel Money as follows;

- • Maximum daily transaction value is Kes 500,000
- • Maximum account balance is Kes 500,000
- • Maximum amount per transaction is Kes 250,000
