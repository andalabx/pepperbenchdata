PesaLink is a payment option offered by KCB where you can affordably move funds from account-to-account, instantly. It is easy to access via your KCB online banking, bank mobile app or USSD.

You can receive money into your KCB Bank account instantly from another bank anytime and will also allow you to send money directly from your KCB Bank account to any local bank account instantly anywhere.

Why use PesaLink?


PesaLink is instant available 24/7 and one can transfer from as low as Kes 10 to as high as Kes 999,999 per single transaction. It is also safe since it eliminates use of cash or cheques as a mode of payment.

Benefits

Instant

Payments are sent instantly to the business or individual bank account.

Accessible

PesaLink is available from your banking channel, just log on and pay.

24/7 Availability

PesaLink is at your service 24/7, including weekends and holidays, even after banking hours.

Hassle-Free

No more waiting in long queues; you can pay for anything, school fees, salaries, rent, suppliers etc. from the comfort of your home or office.

Digital Receipts

Receive a confirmation SMS with a link to a digital receipt, which you can easily share as proof of payment.

Higher Limits Compared To Other Instant Options

Send up to Kes 999,999 per single transaction.

How to Access PesaLink*

On USSD

  • Dial *522#
  • Select Send Money
  • Select PesaLink Instant Transfer
  • Follow the prompts
  • Confirm the payment

On the APP

  • Open your KCB App.
  • Select PesaLink under Cash Transfers
  • Select Send to Other Bank or Mobile
  • Search and select the beneficiary bank where the receiver's account is domiciled.
  • Enter the recipient account number, recipient account name, amount & submit.
  • Enter the Mobi PIN and confirm.
You will receive 2 SMS’s, one from KCB and another one from PESALINK confirming the transaction. The message from PesaLink will also contain the link to a digital receipt as proof of payment being successful.

PesaLink is the easiest way for people and businesses to transfer money instantly account-to- account 24/7/365. You can use your KCB online banking, bank mobile app or USSD to do a bank transfer directly to a bank account in any bank and you don’t need to move funds to mobile money.

All your PesaLink transactions appear in your bank statements, or the transaction history on your online banking and mobile app.

PesaLink is the instant and secure way to pay from account-to-account. Just follow these 5 easy steps:
Open your online banking, mobile app or USSD and select PesaLink from the menu.
Choose from the ‘send to phone’ or ‘send to account’ options.
Enter the phone number or account details, the amount, and a reference.
Confirm the name, total amount and transaction fee and then make the payment.
Check the instant notification (on SMS or App) on the transaction status. Contact your bank immediately if it doesn’t look right.

PesaLink is the instant payments network offered by banks. With PesaLink you can affordably move funds account-to-account, instantly. You can use your online banking, bank mobile app or USSD to transfer – and you don’t need to move funds to mobile money.

The PesaLink network can process transactions from Kes 10 to Kes 999,999. You may have other limits in your account security settings. Contact your bank to increase your daily transaction limits if you need to.

If you’re a business account holder at one of our partners, you can use PesaLink for any instant account-to-account payments to people and businesses i.e. paying rent, school fees, salary payments, NHIF payments, E-citizen payments, supplier payments and any other business payments.

PesaLink is designed to be more affordable than other forms of digital payments. Fees will vary by the amount and are determined by the bank. There’s the added value of instant settlement and fewer intermediaries.

You can use PesaLink for any instant account-to-account transactions to people and businesses anytime even after the bank is closed, on weekends and public holidays.

With PesaLink’s easy steps, mistakes are rare. If you make a mistake or see a payment that you don’t recognise, contact your bank immediately.

You do not need an app. You access PesaLink via your KCB online banking, bank mobile app or USSD.

Most leading banks offer bulk payments to their business customers. With PesaLink these can be sent account-to-account any time, day or night. There is the added value of instant settle- ment and fewer intermediaries.

We are in discussions with our partners and the regulator about opening the PesaLink platform for several new instant account-to-account payment types. We will provide further updates as soon as we can.

These are cookies are created temporarily in your browser's subfolder while you are visiting a website. These cookies expire and are automatically deleted when you close your internet browser.

They remain in your browser’s subfolder and are activated again once you visit the website that created that particular cookie. A persistent cookie does not expire when you close your browser, it remains in the browser’s subfolder for the duration period set within the cookie’s file.

These are cookies used specifically for gathering data on how you use our website e.g., which pages of the website are visited most often, or if you get error messages on web pages. These cookies monitor only the performance of the site as the user interacts with it. These cookies don’t collect identifiable information on visitors, which means all the data collected is anonymous and only used to improve the functionality of a website.

Functionality cookies allow KCB Group website to remember the user's site preferences and choices they make on the site including username, region, and language.
