# Bank on simple fees in 2026



What you see is what you pay. We keep fees simple to beat complex banking.

[Download our fees](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/globalassets/pages/documents-library/transact/personal-banking-fees-september-2026.pdf) [View our fees](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/personal/transact/transact-fees/#transactfees) [Calculate Your Fees](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/personal/transact/transact-fees/#bankcalculator)

![Fees 2026 Retail Desktop Hero](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/contentassets/eed78cef492949dea161991fd2436f9d/fees-2026-retail-desktop-hero.jpg)

Simpler banking starts with affordable fees that make sense. That’s an easier way to manage your money that 25 million South Africans bank on.

### 2026 key fees:

- **R1** Capitec to Capitec payment
- **R2** Payment to other SA banks
- **R3** Debit order
- **R6** Immediate payment to all SA banks
- **R10** Cash withdrawals (per R1 000) at any bank’s ATM in SA

Monthly fee on the Main Account stays the same – **R7.50**

Valid from 1 March 2026.

### What’s new?

- Smart ID (service fee) – **R10**
- New Entrepreneur Card issued in branch – **R70**

### What’s changed?

- Voucher fee is changing from **R2** to **R3**
- Balance enquiry on International Mastercard Machine is changing from **R3** to **R2**

Please refer to the full pricing guide below.
