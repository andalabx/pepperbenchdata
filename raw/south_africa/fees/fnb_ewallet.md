### Make a purchase at selected Engen, Shell, Sasol, and TotalEnergies fuel stations


Dial \*120\*277\*4# and follow the steps. You will automatically receive a one-time PIN (OTP) and a reference number. Note: the OTP expires after 16 hours.



Tell the cashier or fuel attendant you would like to purchase fuel and/or goods at the convenience store. The cashier or fuel attendant will ask for your cellphone number. Enter the OTP and reference number you received from FNB in the card machine.
